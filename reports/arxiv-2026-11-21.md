### 🚀 1. Star decomposition of complete multipartite graphs
- 👤 **Authors**: Juho Lee  
- 🔗 **ArXiv**: [Access Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24973)

#### 📝 Executive Summary & Innovations
- **Main Result**:  
  1. For any integer \(k\ge 3\), the complete graph \(K_n\) admits a \(\{K_{1,k},K_{1,k+1}\}\)-decomposition whenever \(n\ge 2k\); together with the known obstruction \(K_{2k-1}\), this yields the optimal integer threshold \(c_k=4k-3\) for complete graphs.  
  2. In the class of complete multipartite graphs the authors prove that \(\sigma(G)\ge 9\) is sufficient when \(k=3\) (via finite computation and an extension argument), while exhibiting a family \(G_k=K_{k^2-k-1,k-2,1}\) with \(\sigma(G_k)=k^2-1\) that lacks such a decomposition, establishing the lower bound \(c_k\ge k^2\).  
  3. The exact threshold for complete multipartite graphs is therefore \(9\) when \(k=3\); the general sufficiency of \(\sigma(G)\ge k^2\) for \(k\ge4\) remains open.

- **Key Graph Concepts**:  
  - Star decompositions \(\{K_{1,k},K_{1,k+1}\}\)

  - Complete multipartite graphs, complete graphs \(K_n\)

  - Extremal constructions (e.g., \(K_{2k-1}\), \(G_k\))
