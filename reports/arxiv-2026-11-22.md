### 🚀 1. Star decomposition of complete multipartite graphs
- 👤 **Authors**: Juho Lee  
- 🔗 **ArXiv**: [Access Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24973)

#### 📝 Executive Summary & Innovations
- **Main Result**:  
  1. For any integer \(k\ge 3\), the complete graph \(K_n\) admits a \(\{K_{1,k},K_{1,k+1}\}\)-decomposition whenever \(n\ge 2k\); this yields the optimal integer threshold \(c_k=4k-3\) for complete graphs.  
  2.   3. Consequently, the exact threshold for \(k=3\) in the multipartite class is \(9\); the general sufficiency of \(\sigma(G)\ge k^2\) for \(k\ge4\) remains open.
